Fan sound for sleeping

Plays nonstop until you stop it.

Why a fan sound helps you sleep

Many people sleep better with a steady fan sound. The soft, even noise covers sudden sounds and helps your mind relax. If you are used to sleeping with a fan in summer, a fan sound lets you sleep the same way in cold weather, with no wind and no cold air.

On cold winter nights you may not want a real fan, but silence can make it hard to sleep. This fan sound gives you the same sleeping sound with no cold wind.

Press Start fan, choose low, medium or high speed, and set the volume. The sound plays all night until you stop it, or you can set a timer.
